Use TaskBloom with Claude

TaskBloom helps you organize personal tasks and projects. The Claude connector can read and update the TaskBloom data available to the account you authorize.

Connect

  1. Add TaskBloom from Claude's Connectors directory.
  2. Sign in to TaskBloom through the OAuth page and review the access request.
  3. Return to Claude and ask it to list a project or show upcoming tasks.

What you can ask

  • List projects and tasks, filter by status, project, or date, and search task titles.
  • Review completed work, task history, recurring tasks, and eligible workspace details.
  • Create or update tasks and projects, complete tasks, and manage recurring work.
  • Review Bloom Pet profiles and companion preferences.

Safety and access

The connector follows the permissions of the TaskBloom account you sign in with. Claude may ask you to confirm actions that change data. Review the action before approving it. Never share passwords, access tokens, or private credentials in a prompt or support request.

The connector does not accept payment details or initiate purchases. Paid subscriptions are handled separately in the TaskBloom Android app through Google Play.
