TaskBloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

KIM MING XUAN, operating as Bloom Apps (“we”), provides TaskBloom and the shared Bloom account. This policy explains how TaskBloom information is handled. Contact thomaskim.info@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we handle

  • Account information: account identifier, email address, sign-in provider details, authentication and session records, and account preferences.
  • TaskBloom content: task and project names, descriptions, status, dates, recurring-task rules, notes, activity history, workspace membership and invitations, and Bloom Pets adoption, progress, preferences, and memories associated with your TaskBloom workspaces.
  • Shared-workspace information: workspace membership, roles, invitations, and changes made by collaborators. Other members can see information you add to a shared workspace.
  • Subscription and transaction information: Google Play purchase verification data, subscription state, product and plan identifiers, and transaction references. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.
  • Service and security information: request time, IP/network information, app or browser details, error diagnostics, authentication and security events, and MCP tool activity needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Support information: messages and account details you choose to include in support, data-copy, or deletion requests.
  • Device copies: the app may keep local or offline task data and widget snapshots on your device to provide its features.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in; store and sync tasks, projects, and workspace data; provide Bloom Pets features; process subscriptions; enforce account and workspace permissions; respond to support and privacy requests; prevent abuse; secure the service; and diagnose operational failures.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use customer data to train AI models.

Hosting and service providers

  • Bloom Apps: Bloom Auth, the Bloom API, and the primary database are hosted on a GreenCloud VPS in Singapore. The service uses self-hosted authentication and PostgreSQL. User content is not hosted in a Supabase-managed production project.
  • Google: Google Sign-In is available when you choose it. Google Play Billing is used for applicable subscriptions and purchase verification. When you use TaskBloom through Gemini, Google receives the prompt, tool inputs, and results needed for that request and handles them under its privacy policy.
  • Resend: delivers authentication and account emails. We verified that our account is on Resend’s Free plan. Resend states that while an account is active it retains email and log data for 30 days and backup copies for 7 days. Resend stores customer data in the United States. See Resend’s retention and location details.
  • Google Drive: stores encrypted offsite backups. Backup contents are encrypted by Bloom Apps before upload; names and timestamps of backup folders may remain visible to the storage provider. The Google Drive storage region is not selected by our backup configuration. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • OpenAI / ChatGPT: if you connect TaskBloom to ChatGPT and use an MCP tool, ChatGPT receives the prompt, tool inputs, and results needed for that request. OpenAI handles that information under its privacy policy.
  • Anthropic / Claude: if you connect TaskBloom to Claude and use an MCP tool, Anthropic receives the prompt, tool inputs, and results needed for that request. Anthropic handles that information under its privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: Cloudflare provides DNS for our domains. GitHub Pages hosts public support, terms, consent, and privacy pages. They may process ordinary connection data when you visit those pages.

These providers receive information only as needed to deliver their services, protect them, or comply with law. Providers may process information outside Singapore; their storage and transfer practices are described in their linked policies.

AI assistant connections (MCP)

TaskBloom can connect to AI assistants that support its MCP server, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini when those integrations are available. When you authorize an assistant and use a TaskBloom tool, the assistant provider receives the prompt, tool inputs, and TaskBloom information returned for that request. This can include task and project details, recurring-task rules, activity history, or shared-workspace information, depending on the tools you use and your account permissions. If Bloom Pet tools are available and you use them, the response may also include related Bloom Pet information. Access is tied to the Bloom account you authorize. Disconnect TaskBloom in the connected assistant’s settings to withdraw that connection. The assistant provider handles prompts and tool data under its own privacy terms. Do not include information you do not want that provider to process. Bloom Apps does not use customer content to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and TaskBloom content while your account remains active. You can request TaskBloom-only deletion, WeekBloom-only deletion, or deletion of the full Bloom account from the app or the public account-deletion page. We verify the signed-in account and process verified deletion requests within 30 days; in-app requests are normally applied immediately. Product-only deletion preserves the shared Bloom sign-in and the other app’s data. Full-account deletion removes both apps’ data and Bloom sign-in.

Deleting an owned shared workspace removes its content for its members. Product-only deletion also removes your membership from other people’s shared workspaces. A deletion request revokes access to the affected app, but does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel recurring billing separately in Google Play; billing may continue until you cancel it.

Encrypted backups are retained for no more than 28 days and are not used for routine service access. A backup may contain a copy of deleted data until that backup expires. Local copies on a device that is offline may remain until the app reconnects and clears its data, or you remove the app’s local storage. Resend’s email/log data is retained for up to 30 days on our current plan; its backup copies persist for 7 days. Transaction records may be kept longer when needed for accounting, refunds, fraud prevention, or legal requirements.

Diagnostic logs are retained for up to 14 days. Authentication and security audit records are retained for up to 30 days. These short retention periods do not apply to your task history while your account is active or to transaction records retained for the purposes above.

Access, copies, and choices

You can request a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct it, or ask a privacy question by emailing thomaskim.info@gmail.com. We verify account control before providing a copy or acting on a request. There is no self-service export at this time. You can disconnect an AI assistant connection in that assistant’s settings and can sign out of Bloom Apps on your devices.

Security

We use HTTPS for network connections, server-side account and product authorization, restricted access to the backend, and encrypted backup files before offsite upload. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

People aged 13–17 may use TaskBloom. We do not market TaskBloom to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us at thomaskim.info@gmail.com.

International processing and changes

Primary Bloom Apps data is hosted in Singapore. Resend stores customer data in the United States. Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, and GitHub may process information in other countries. We link to provider privacy information above so you can review their practices. We may update this policy when our services or practices change; the date at the top shows when this version was last revised.
