Privacy Policy — TaskBloom and WeekBloom

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Bloom Apps ("we", "us") operates the TaskBloom and WeekBloom apps and their related services, including the Bloom sign-in and the MCP connectors that let AI assistants read and change your data with your permission.

1. Who we are

Controller: Thomas Kim (individual developer), Malaysia.

Contact for privacy questions: thomaskim.info@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • **Account data:** email address, display name, and the sign-in provider you used (email and password, or Google).
  • **Google sign-in:** when you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name and profile identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • **App content you create:** tasks, projects, recurring tasks, timetables, courses, events, schedule rules and temporary overrides, workspace and invitation records, and pet data if you use Bloom Pets.
  • **Usage and security data:** sign-in events, session tokens (stored in hashed form where possible), and error logs needed to keep the service secure and working.
  • **Connector data:** when you connect an AI assistant through our MCP connectors, the requests it makes on your behalf and the results returned to it.
  • **Billing records:** plan, trial and membership status. Payments are arranged manually and verified by the owner. We do not store card numbers or bank details.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To provide the features you use: tasks, timetables, sharing with the people you invite, reminders and widgets.
  • To run the MCP connectors you authorise.
  • To manage memberships, trials and entitlements.
  • To troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Who can see your data

  • You, and people you explicitly share a workspace or timetable with, according to the permissions you set.
  • Our service providers who host and operate the service: our self-hosted server in Singapore, Google (sign-in) and Google Drive (encrypted off-site backups). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. Where data is stored

Our primary database and application servers run on a self-hosted server in Singapore. Encrypted backups are stored off-site in Google Drive. Data stored in Singapore and Google Drive is transferred outside Malaysia.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and app data: while your account is active.
  • Deleted items: 30 days for recovery, then removed from active systems.
  • Backups: encrypted backups are kept for 60 days and then deleted.
  • Sign-in and security logs: 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or export your data from within the app, or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account: email us at the contact address below and we will delete it.
  • Revoke an MCP connector at any time by contacting us.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (for example to object or to lodge a complaint with a regulator). Contact us to exercise them.

8. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and per-user data separation. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you believe your account was misused.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes we will tell you in the app or by email.
